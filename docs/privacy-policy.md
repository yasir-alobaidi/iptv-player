# Bijou privacy policy

*Effective 27 October 2026.*

Bijou is a video player for Android (phones, tablets, Chromebooks and
TVs), Linux and Windows, made by `[developer name, as on Google Play]`.
It comes with nothing to watch of its own: it plays the playlists,
program guides and services you add to it. This policy says what Bijou
does with your information. In short: **it stays on your device, and
nothing is ever sent to us.**

## What Bijou keeps on your device

- **The sources you add:** their names and addresses, and for a server
  you sign in to, your username and password. The password is stored
  encrypted (on Android, with a key held in the Android Keystore).
- **What those sources contain**, as Bijou downloads it for you: the
  lists of channels, movies and series, the program guide, artwork.
- **What you do in Bijou:** favorites, hidden categories, watch history
  and where you stopped, and your settings.
- **A log of what Bijou did**, to explain a problem if you ask. Passwords
  and addresses that contain sign-in details are removed before anything
  is written to it.

All of it is in Bijou's private storage on your device. We (the people
who make Bijou) never receive any of it.

## What leaves your device

Bijou connects only to the addresses you give it, because you gave them:
the server you sign in to (which receives your username and password, as
its sign-in requires), the playlist and guide links you add, and the
streams and pictures they point to. Those services are not ours; what
they do with your information is up to them and their own policies.

Many of those services use plain `http`, which isn't encrypted on the
way. Bijou uses whatever your service offers; prefer `https` addresses
when your service has them.

On a TV, **Send to phone** shares Bijou's report (with passwords and
sign-in details removed) with a phone on your home network, only while
you keep that screen open.

**Bijou has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash
reporting service.** It sends nothing to us and shares nothing with
anyone else.

## Children

Bijou isn't made for children. It plays whatever the sources you add
contain and doesn't filter it.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it:

- remove a source in **Settings → Sources** (its password goes with it);
- clear artwork, the guide or your watch history in **Settings → Data**;
- or uninstall Bijou, which deletes everything it kept.

As we hold none of your information, there is nothing for us to delete;
ask us anyway if you have a question.

## Changes

If Bijou ever starts handling information differently, this policy will
say so before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
